The art site is for finished work. This one is for the drills that sit underneath it: putting solids in space, checking vanishing points, and writing down what the drawing was trying to teach.

Two piles:

1. **Form construction** — the plates. Image first, a short note if there is something worth keeping.
2. **Drafting meta** — the thinking around the plates. Longer, dated, like a journal.

I am not trying to make either of them look impressive. I am trying to make them easy to add to, and easy to look back through.
