---
title: "Why boxes first"
date: "2026-08-20"
excerpt: "Form construction is easier when everything starts as a box, even when the subject is not a box."
---

A box is the cheapest way to put something in perspective. It has six faces, all rectangles, and the vanishing points do all of the work. Once a box is sitting correctly on the ground plane, you can cut it, wrap it, or stack on it without inventing a new spatial system.

That is why the form construction plates start there. The drawing is not of a box. The box is the scaffolding.

If a later form looks wrong, the first question is whether the box it came from was honest.
