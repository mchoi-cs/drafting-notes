---
title: "A new direction"
date: "2026-09-25"
excerpt: "Toward naïve art: childlike clarity and purposeful abstraction, while still sharpening drafting."
---

I want to start experimenting with naïve art. By that I mean childlike clarity and purposeful abstraction.

For a long time I honed technique without a clear sense of how it connected to my intentions. That sounds a little hyperbolic. I have made a lot of narrative work. Still, I want art that is compelling and fun for me: an exercise in focus, and a reflection of my life.

Naïve art feels like the way forward. At the same time, I still want to strengthen my drafting. There is something sophisticated about someone who clearly understands 3D form and the dense technique that goes with it, then shows what they choose to abstract away versus what they keep. That selectivity is very appealing to me.

Laboring over little details can mean you do not know what you really want to say. Realism sometimes feels alienating. This is a new comprehension for me, because most of my art journey was doodling, not drafting.

I have some notes on computer vision I will post elsewhere. If you want the working trail, the drafting notes and 3D models live on GitHub: [drafting-notes](https://github.com/mchoi-cs/drafting-notes). Plates are also up at [charminglines](https://charminglines.vercel.app).
